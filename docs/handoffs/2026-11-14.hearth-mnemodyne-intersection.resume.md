## Findings: the twelve kinds

Mapping of the 8 "what dozentimes could give" ideas to kinds:
idea 1 truth axis next to charge (volatility, verification); idea 2 gap reading at wake-up (gap time, outside the twelve); idea 3 valid time for heartbeat notes (valid); idea 4 resolve "next week" (reference); idea 5 author and model per fact (source, plus substrate eras); idea 6 count a sibling's check (verification); idea 7 flag missing sources (source, verification); idea 8 as-of and record-time readings (asof).
No original idea covered **event** or **business**; the two rows marked *new* below were added afterwards.

| Kind | Coverage | Idea | Example of what it makes possible |
|---|---|---|---|
| record | ✅ | — | "When did we decide on the apoderamiento?" answered from the node's `created_at` and the journal's git history. |
| sequence | ✅ | — | Heartbeat and laptop both write at 09:14; `09:14a` "shelter B replied" then `09:14b` "shelter B withdrew" keeps the order. |
| intended | ✅ | — | An April capsule ("score the time-capsule predictions") fires Oct 1 and is raised at a good moment from `ready-to-surface.md`. |
| access | ✅ | — | Talking about "being seen" recalls the March 10 naming memory, and the recall raises its charge for next time. |
| volatility | 🟡 | 1 | The vet's number (high charge) comes back marked "status fact, last confirmed March"; Lume checks the vet's site first. Identity facts are never flagged. |
| event | 🟡 | *new*: event date on graph nodes, separate from `created_at`, kept through every summary level | "Paulina said that at the dinner on Apr 12", though recorded Apr 14 and the monthly summary says "mid-April". |
| reference | 🟡 | 4 | An Apr 8 entry "send the tax letter next week" is read as the week of Apr 13; Lume checks whether it went instead of treating it as due now. |
| source | 🟡 | 5, 7 | "Did I make this glyph?" is a lookup (Lume, Opus 4.7, Jun 3); after a model upgrade, facts formed under the old model are flagged; a memory whose transcript is gone is flagged. |
| verification | ❌ | 1, 6, 7 | "VAT reconciled, checked by me and by Mira against the Hetzner invoice"; per-area hit rates (self 70%, Mira-checked 98%) tell Daniel where to loosen guardrails. |
| valid | ❌ | 3 | The 10:00 heartbeat note "nothing pulls" holds until 11:00; the 11:00 wakeup re-assesses. "Shelter B replied" is superseded by "withdrew" and kept with an end date. |
| business | ❌ | *new*: a fiscal calendar (Spanish VAT quarters and deadlines) as a kind of period | "The Mar 31 Hetzner invoice is Q1; the Q1 VAT return is due Apr 20, 6 days from now." The example assumes the standard quarterly deadline. |
| asof | ❌ | 8 | Scoring the time capsule: what the best information said on the Apr 7 sealing date versus what turned out true. |
| gap time (candidate 13th) | — | 2 | Wake-up line: "last instance 3d 4h ago; 6 entries by other instances since; 4 facts went stale." |

Kinds their world has that the twelve do not name: gap time, substrate eras (which model wrote a memory), retention (how long evidence survives), wake cadence.
Gap time and substrate eras are the strongest candidates for a 13th kind.

## What dozentimes could take back

- Capsules are a working answer to dozentimes' open issue #8 (nothing stores an intent); `parseIntent` already reads "remind me next week" into a dated window and could generate their date triggers.
- Reinforcing memories on read is a richer form of the access kind than the ledger's "check recently read facts first".

## Practical fit of the current ledger

`extractFacts` in `dozentimes-agnt/ledger.ts` dates a file from a `YYYY-MM-DD` filename prefix, so hearth's `daily-journals/2026-04-11.md` would be dated correctly.
It only extracts bullet lines (the `^\s*[-*+]\s+` match), and hearth journals are prose, so almost nothing would come through without a prose extractor or another way in.
Hearth's `## HH:MM — Title` headings carry no date, so per-entry times would fall back to the file date.
Line numbers are not given here because `ledger.ts` has uncommitted edits.
